# Design: 007-auth

Follows `spec/DESIGN.md` tokens. Layout and visual structure come from the Myanmar Lottery artifact page for this section; all colors use theme tokens (no literal hex in components), spacing mobile-first.

## Layout

Full-bleed accent surface. From `lg`: brand panel on the left (ticket icon, welcome title, three benefit checks) and the form card on the right; on phones the brand header stacks above the card. Card holds the Log in / Sign up tabs, fields, "or" divider and two social buttons side by side.
